There are six types of food that people with psoriasis should avoid: firstly, foods high in refined sugar or natural sugar; secondly, processed foods or foods high in essential oils, such as canned meat, canned fish, or fried foods; thirdly, foods with many spices, such as pepper, chili, and ginger; fourthly, foods containing a lot of chocolate or eggs, because eating too much of these foods will worsen the disease; fifthly, alcohol, which people with psoriasis should limit as much as possible; and sixthly, red meat, such as beef or dog meat, which is best eaten with poultry or shellfish like salmon.
