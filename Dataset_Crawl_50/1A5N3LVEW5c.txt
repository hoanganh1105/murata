When things get difficult, we usually resort to skin biopsy . How can psoriasis be distinguished from other skin diseases? In psoriasis, the basic characteristic of a psoriasis lesion is redness and scaling on the affected skin area, with a fairly clear boundary. To distinguish it from other lesions, it is often found to have thickened skin and peeling. However, in clinical practice, for specialists with psoriasis, determining whether or not a patient has the condition is not difficult at all. It is often possible to differentiate it from conditions such as psoriasis, nummular eczema, localized psoriasis, and periodontal disease. When faced with difficult situations, we usually perform a skin analysis, examining the skin lesions, which are very specific and different from other types of lesions.
