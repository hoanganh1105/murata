Psoriasis is a skin disease with many causes, such as bacterial infections, parasitic infections, and recently, a viral infection that is currently attracting worldwide attention. Furthermore, psoriasis is often hereditary and can be caused by a weakened immune system, such as metabolic or degenerative diseases, especially in older people. The symptoms of psoriasis manifest as a skin condition that is not contagious, but it can become chronic. If not treated properly, psoriasis can cause generalized erythema or other complications, particularly in older people or those with underlying conditions, which can worsen their condition. Some young people no longer want to start a family because every time they see the scars on their bodies, they feel very stressed.
