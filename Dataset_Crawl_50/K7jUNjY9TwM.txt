Can we detect psoriasis early in children and prevent it from progressing ? Detecting psoriasis in children can be problematic because, for example, psoriatic lesions typically start on the scalp, which is similar in children to adults. Initially, if there is a red, scaly lesion, experienced specialists might suspect it and then diagnose and treat it. There are two ways: one is to try treatment, and the second is to also purchase a medical diagnosis. However, in children, we rarely do biopsies; we just treat the condition because children don't usually have psychological factors or other issues. They often have scalp psoriasis, atopic dermatitis, or oily skin, so the treatment needs to be similar. Therefore, we choose a method that is effective, safe, and has few side effects for young children.
