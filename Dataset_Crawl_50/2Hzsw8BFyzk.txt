Aloe vera and Houttuynia cordata are very effective in treating psoriasis and skin rashes . Many people with psoriasis have dry, irritated, and painful skin that is very uncomfortable. We take about 40-50g of aloe vera, remove the peel and the slimy outer layer, and keep the inner pulp. We take about 40 to 50 grams of Houttuynia cordata leaves. We put these two things together. We can blend it into juice and drink it daily to help cool the body, detoxify, soothe the liver, moisturize the lungs, improve the appearance of our skin , reduce psoriasis, and effectively prevent fungal infections. Anyone suffering from psoriasis can try this simple and effective method.
