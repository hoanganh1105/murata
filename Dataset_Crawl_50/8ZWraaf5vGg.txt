I have psoriasis, can I just buy topical medication to cure it? Hello Phuong Anh, many patients have the same question as you. It's true that in some cases of mild psoriasis, topical medication can alleviate the symptoms. However, topical medication only provides temporary relief and doesn't control the psoriasis itself. Some patients have used overdosing medication without a doctor's prescription, leading to skin irritation, itching, and burning. In some cases, overdosing can lead to serious reactions such as a full-body rash and difficulty breathing. Therefore, using only topical medication cannot control psoriasis. When psoriasis is first detected, it's best to treat it as early as possible to prevent its spread and development, reduce symptoms like burning, itching, and peeling skin, and avoid health problems such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and hypertension. Therefore, when you have psoriasis, you should not self-treat it with medication but go to reputable facilities for examination and appropriate treatment by specialists.
