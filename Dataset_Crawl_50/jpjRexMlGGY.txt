Dear medical professionals, according to information from the Ho Chi Minh City Dermatology Hospital, recently, this unit has received many cases of severe complications requiring hospitalization due to self-medication with psoriasis drugs purchased from unreliable sources on social media. To avoid serious complications, doctors advise patients to seek examination and treatment at specialized dermatology facilities and not to follow advertisements on social media or to self-medicate with unverified oral or topical medications without a dermatologist's prescription. If the generalized redness of the skin due to psoriasis persists, it can lead to dangerous complications such as sepsis, pneumonia, or heart failure, which can be fatal. In particular, some psoriasis patients may develop psoriatic arthritis during the course of the disease, with a very high risk of disability. Psoriasis is a chronic disease that severely affects the quality of life of patients and their families. This disease is not contagious through direct or indirect contact.
