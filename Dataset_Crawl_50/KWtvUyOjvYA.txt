Foods that are good for psoriasis patients: People with psoriasis should eat plenty of foods containing Omega 3 because it helps slow down the aging process, for example, mackerel and tuna. Secondly, we should eat plenty of fruits and vegetables containing beta-carotene, such as carrots and tomatoes. Finally, eating plenty of black sesame seeds is very good for psoriasis patients because black sesame seeds contain a lot of vitamin E and Omega 3.
