If you have psoriasis, what foods should you avoid? First, you should avoid foods that commonly cause allergies, such as meat, fish, eggs, and dairy. Second, avoid exposure to ultraviolet rays and sunlight. Third, avoid insomnia and stress, because frequent insomnia or stress can worsen the condition. Fourth, limit the use of products containing Coco-Isophosphate because frequent use can easily lead to relapses and many unwanted side effects.
