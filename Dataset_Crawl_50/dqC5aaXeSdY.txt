Moving on to other information: To commemorate World Psoriasis Day on the morning of October 29th, the Ho Chi Minh City Dermatology Hospital organized a club activity program for psoriasis patients. Participating patients received free consultations from doctors about their psoriasis, were screened for risk factors for psoriasis, and received stress management training to improve their quality of life. The hospital's expectation is to provide the most accurate and best information to patients, create a network of interaction among patients so they can share information and data for better care, and also facilitate regular communication with medical staff, specialists, and even leading experts in the field when not needed, to provide them with the best knowledge to self-care and minimize complications. Currently, no one knows the exact cause of psoriasis, but it is believed to be related to certain factors. The genetic makeup of psoriasis, while seemingly frightening, is completely non-contagious, so we should not discriminate against or shun psoriasis patients.
