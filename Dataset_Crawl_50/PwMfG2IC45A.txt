Yes, yes [music] Psoriasis is one of the common dermatological diseases, usually manifesting as red, scaly patches of skin. However, the skin is not the only organ affected; psoriasis is a systemic inflammatory disease that affects many organs. The joints are one of the main areas where psoriasis causes damage, known as psoriatic arthritis. Most patients will experience symptoms first, and only later will they develop psoriatic arthritis. However, some patients may have joint symptoms before the skin appears, so we may overlook psoriatic arthritis or misdiagnose it as another type of arthritis. Joint pain, swelling, and joint deformity are the main symptoms of psoriatic arthritis. The disease can affect any joint in the body, including the fingertips, toes, and spine, ranging from mild to severe, including swelling, pain, joint destruction, and permanent deformity, severely reducing quality of life. Therefore, screening and detection are crucial. Early detection of psoriatic arthritis is essential. Several factors may suggest psoriatic arthritis, including symptoms of psoriasis on the fingers and toes, swelling and pain, nail symptoms such as alcohol nails, fractures, and X-ray images showing new bone formation near the joints. Early screening and diagnosis will help treat psoriatic arthritis effectively and avoid disabling sequelae later on.
