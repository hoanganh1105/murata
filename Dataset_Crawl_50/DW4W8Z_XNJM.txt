The nature of this disease is a weakened immune system, a reduced resistance to disease. Therefore, other external diseases develop under the same conditions, and it's easily confused with other conditions. Accurate diagnosis and treatment by the right doctor, the right medicine, and the right specialist are crucial for positive results. For example, psoriasis, as I mentioned above, can develop into a pustular form, causing inflammation and pus formation, especially on the hands and feet, and in some cases, especially on the nails. Given our country's conditions – humid and dusty environments – infection and inflammation are common. It's crucial to diagnose and differentiate between these conditions, and secondly, to understand the stage of psoriasis development in order to prevent it promptly and use appropriate medications.
