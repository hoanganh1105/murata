Actually, when women have many illnesses during pregnancy, they need to be very careful because it affects the fetus. It's not just psoriasis; generally, during pregnancy, a woman's immune system is not the same as before pregnancy. Therefore, when psoriasis develops during pregnancy, it's seen that it can have an impact. So, during pregnancy, especially when using vitamins to promote cell regeneration as I mentioned above, one must be very careful, especially with long-term use of vitamin A, and should not be abused. Corticosteroids, in particular, are contraindicated, and even some medications of traditional medicine origin need to be used cautiously during pregnancy.
