What to eat when suffering from psoriasis? Psoriasis is a chronic skin disease that can significantly affect a person's quality of life due to skin symptoms and associated inflammation. Although there are treatments to alleviate psoriasis symptoms, dietary changes can be a valuable supplementary method to effectively manage this condition. Below are the types of foods that psoriasis patients should eat and avoid. Nutritional factors for psoriasis include: Processed foods often contain unhealthy trans fats and high levels of sodium, which can worsen inflammation and negatively impact overall health. Foods high in saturated fats, such as red meat and full-fat dairy products, can contribute to inflammation and should be consumed in moderation. Additionally, consuming sugary foods can lead to increased inflammation and may worsen psoriasis symptoms. Patients should avoid sugary snacks, sugary drinks, and processed foods. Instead of sugar, choose natural sweeteners like honey in moderation when needed. One of the main dietary recommendations for patients is to include gluten-free foods. According to Dr. Pradit Mahan, a researcher and founder of regenerative medicine solutions in India, gluten, a protein found in wheat, barley, and rye, can cause inflammation in some people, worsening symptoms. Therefore, this doctor also recommends anti- inflammatory foods to include in the diet to reduce inflammation and support the immune system. First, foods rich in antioxidants: berries, leafy green vegetables, and colorful fruits and vegetables contain many antioxidants that can help neutralize harmful free radicals and reduce inflammation. Next are Omega-3 fatty acids: fatty fish such as salmon, mackerel, and sardines are sources of Omega-3 fatty acids with anti-inflammatory properties. These can help alleviate psoriasis symptoms. Foods containing phytochemicals, such as turmeric, ginger, and green tea, have anti-inflammatory effects and may contribute to controlling disease symptoms.
