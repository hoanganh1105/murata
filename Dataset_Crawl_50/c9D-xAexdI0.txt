So, psoriasis is a chronic disease with many forms and different manifestations, and it cannot be completely cured. The causes of the disease are often genetic or other factors such as skin infections, trauma, stress, drug use, alcohol, stimulants, or corticosteroid-containing medications. Although not life-threatening, it causes discomfort, pain, and a significant psychological burden later on if the patient gives up trying to live with psoriasis. Previously, patients were given skin creams or light therapy. Recently, the hospital has applied a type of biological drug in the form of targeted therapy with long-lasting effects over a cyclical period, helping patients control psoriasis well and improve their quality of life without having to use medication every day. In addition, patients also need to adhere to treatment as directed by their doctor, design a suitable diet and lifestyle.
