When suffering from psoriasis, it's very distressing, and as I mentioned earlier, it affects young people the most, especially women. Many cases involve young women who are not yet married, and when they come for examination, they cry because of how terrible it looks – like a leopard's skin. Even beautiful women are often found with patchy psoriasis lesions, especially older people with underlying health conditions. They are very afraid, some even experiencing depression, itching, insomnia, and other significant disruptions to their lives. Therefore, if psoriasis is treated correctly by a specialist, I believe it will lead to a positive outcome for patients.
