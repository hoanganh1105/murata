In October 2019, during a health talk on common skin diseases in men and a scholarship program for the children of soldiers from the 171st Naval Brigade and the DK1 offshore platform in Vung Tau, organized by the Science and Life newspaper, the Board of Directors of the Ho Chi Minh City Dermatology Hospital was informed about the case of Nguyen Xuan An, son of Comrade Nguyen Xuan Ha, currently working at the DK1 offshore platform, who suffered from psoriasis and had sought treatment everywhere without improvement. Sympathizing with Xuan An's family situation and wanting to help Lieutenant Colonel Nguyen Xuan Ha feel secure in his role of defending the sovereignty of our beloved country's seas and islands, the Board of Directors and the Trade Union of the Ho Chi Minh City Dermatology Hospital called for support from departments, wards, and staff to help Xuan An receive treatment. In total, the hospital contributed 144 million VND from its staff to provide Xuan An with access to the most advanced psoriasis treatment currently available: biological medicine. It helped improve almost all the skin lesions, which helped me live more confidently and integrate with my friends. I saw three or four pieces of the item again, and it seemed like it was completely gone, only a few spots remained. The money was all gone, but at first I started, and I was sincere because I felt a little emotional, and I really missed it. I made more friends than adults, participated more than one person, and supported the child of a naval soldier in treating psoriasis. This activity has profound humanitarian significance, showing the concern of the staff of the Ho Chi Minh City Dermatology Hospital for the difficult circumstances of naval soldiers' families. From there, it contributes to stabilizing the home front and encouraging naval soldiers to confidently stay at sea and guard the islands of our beloved homeland.
