Doctor, can psoriasis be cured by using topical medications and bathing in herbal water ? It depends on the severity because in psoriasis we can categorize it as follows: if it's common psoriasis, it's mild; if it's pustular psoriasis, arthritis, or erythrodermic psoriasis, then just applying creams and bathing alone will never cure it. But with common psoriasis, in its mild form, sometimes only a patch of lesions on the scalp, then you can use topical medication combined with shampooing and care, and it might still stabilize. However, remember to choose the right shampoo and body wash; the principle is not to use random herbal baths, not to scrub vigorously, and not to use excessively hot baths. For mild cases, we can use topical medication. And that's why the strategy we wrote about in that book on psoriasis is to use medication alone, in combination with rotation and sequential treatment. Solitary means just the lighter forms. If I just apply it topically, even just corticosteroids or Divobet, and I multiply the results over time, it should be good. As for the other types of herbal baths, I absolutely must not use them. You must use a bath soap prescribed by your doctor.
